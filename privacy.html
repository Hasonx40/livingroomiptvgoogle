Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 | Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Summary for Livingroom IPTV Player users: We respect your privacy. We do not collect personal information, and we do not host, store, or transmit your media playlists or streaming credentials to any external servers. Everything stays on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile and Android TV application Livingroom IPTV Player ("the Application"), developed for Android TV, Google TV, smartphones, and tablets.

By downloading, installing, or using Livingroom IPTV Player, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

⚠️ Important Content & Copyright Disclaimer

Livingroom IPTV Player does not supply, host, broadcast, or provide any media content, TV channels, films, or stream links. The application is solely a media player tool that allows users to play their own legally obtained M3U playlists and Xtream Codes API accounts. Users are completely responsible for the content they choose to stream.

2. Information We Collect and Store

Our core architecture is built around user privacy. We do not operate remote user registration databases or media servers.

  • No Personal Identification Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or personal location data.
  • Local-Only Storage: Your server URL, username, password (Xtream Codes API), and M3U playlist URLs or files are stored strictly locally on your Android device (using Android encrypted preferences and local storage). We cannot access, view, or transfer this information.
  • Cached Data: Channel logos and EPG metadata requested from your provider are cached locally on your device to optimize load times and can be cleared at any time in the app or system settings.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep Livingroom IPTV Player available and maintained, the Application utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for advertisement delivery and crash analytics:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may access and collect your device's Advertising ID (GAID), coarse device data, IP address, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
    For more details, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

4. Permissions Requested by the Application

Livingroom IPTV Player requests minimal permissions necessary for core media streaming:

  • INTERNET: Required to fetch your streams, video playlists, and sports live scores.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect network connectivity (Wi-Fi / Ethernet / Cellular) and adjust streaming buffer quality.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided any personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Security of Your Data

Because all your playlist credentials and configurations reside locally on your device, their security is tied directly to your device's system security. We recommend keeping your Android OS updated and securing your home network and TV with standard passcodes.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
